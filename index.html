Gesture tips & connection help

Heart: use two hands. Touch index fingertips together above touching thumb tips, with a visible opening between them. Circle: make an “OK” ring with one thumb and index finger; keep the other three fingers extended. Hold either shape for about one second.

The hint is a suggestion: any two matching supported gestures count. Different gestures, missing hands, an unrecognized shape, or a paused camera do not match. This prototype uses hand landmarks and geometric rules, so lighting and hand position can affect recognition.

Use good light and keep your complete hands in view. Each person needs their own device with a camera and microphone. Use headphones to avoid echo. Keep this page visible while matching.

The second device needs the HTTPS invite link. A localhost address only works on this computer.

Camera and microphone start only when you join. Video and audio are shared with the other room participant using WebRTC; hand recognition runs on your device. This app does not record calls. Anyone with your invite link can join the room’s waiting pool. You only share a call with your current partner. Press Skip person to end that call and connect to the next available participant. If nobody is available, you’ll wait. Skipped partners will not be paired again during the same room session.
